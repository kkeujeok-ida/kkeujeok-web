Kkeujeok - Handwriting Notes

What is Kkeujeok

Kkeujeok is a handwriting note app. It is available as an Android app and a web app, and it is currently free.

Notes live in your Google Drive

Sign in with your Google account and your notes are saved to your own Google Drive and synced across your devices. The app asks for one Drive permission, drive.file, so it only works with files Kkeujeok created. The operator cannot access your notes.
