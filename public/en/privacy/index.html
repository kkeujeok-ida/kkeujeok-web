Kkeujeok Privacy Policy

Your notes stay in your Google Drive

Notes are stored only in your own Google Drive. The operator cannot access your notes.

Permission we ask for

Kkeujeok asks for one Google Drive permission, drive.file. It only works with files Kkeujeok created and cannot see other files in your Drive.

Servers and data collection

Kkeujeok has no server. It uses no analytics and no crash reporting.

Encryption

Kkeujeok does not separately encrypt the note files in your Drive.

Disconnecting and revoking access

Disconnecting in Settings revokes Kkeujeok's Drive access. You can also revoke it at any time on your Google Account permissions page. Note files in your Drive stay as they are.
