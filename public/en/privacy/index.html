Kkeujeok Privacy Policy

Scope and effective date

This policy applies to Kkeujeok, a handwriting note app (the Android app and the web app, "the app"). It is effective from November 16, 2026. The app is operated by the Kkeujeok development team.

Google user data the app accesses

The app uses Google Sign-In to read your account's unique identifier (sub) and email address, and it asks for one Google Drive permission, drive.file. That permission only covers files Kkeujeok created or that you open with the app. The app cannot see other files in your Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the features you ask for: saving notes to your Drive, loading them, syncing them across your devices, and showing which account is signed in. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing and transfer

The operator does not receive your Google user data. The app talks to Google directly from your device or browser. Your data is not sold, transferred, or disclosed to third parties. Exporting or sharing a note happens only when you choose to do it.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Kkeujeok's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising. We do not sell it to or share it with data brokers or information resellers. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. We do not use it to build separate databases. No human reads your notes, because the operator never receives them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AI and machine learning

Information obtained through Google Workspace APIs is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized (non-personalized) AI or machine-learning models. The app also has no AI feature that analyzes your notes.

Where data is stored and how it is protected

Notes are stored only in your own Google Drive. Kkeujeok has no server, and it uses no analytics and no crash reporting. Communication with Google uses HTTPS. In the web app, the access token lives only in browser memory and is not saved. Google Drive stores and protects the note files. Kkeujeok does not separately encrypt them.

Information kept on your device

For offline use and fast display, the app may keep temporary copies of your notes on your device or in your browser. The web app also keeps your account identifier and email in local storage to remember that this browser signed in before. Signing out or disconnecting clears that mark.

Retention and deletion

Kkeujeok keeps no user data on the operator side. Your notes stay in your Drive until you delete them there. To delete a note, delete it in Google Drive. Because the operator holds no data, there is nothing to request deletion of. Temporary copies on your device go away when you uninstall the app or clear the browser site data.

Disconnecting and revoking access

Disconnecting in Settings revokes Kkeujeok's Drive access. You can also revoke it at any time on your Google Account permissions page. Note files in your Drive stay as they are.
